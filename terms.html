Customer and companion terms

Terms of Use.

Clear boundaries for a managed, non-medical companionship service in Trinidad and Tobago.

Effective date: 22 September 2026

Important: Just Company is not a dating service, medical provider, nursing service, therapist, emergency responder, transport provider or residential-care service. These terms should be reviewed by Trinidad and Tobago counsel before final reliance.

1. The service

Just Company coordinates paid, non-medical companionship for adults of different ages and life stages. Services may include conversation, shared time, supported walks, cafés, reading, games, hobbies and approved cultural or community activities. Just Company remains the customer-facing coordinator and reviews requests privately.

2. Who may use the service

  • Customers must be adults and provide truthful contact and verification information.
  • A customer may enquire for themselves, a family member or someone they support, but must have authority to make the request and must provide accurate information about the person attending.
  • Companion applicants must be at least 18, live in Trinidad and Tobago, complete screening and accept the Independent Companion Services Agreement.
  • Just Company may refuse, pause or end an enquiry, application or booking where suitability, safety, capacity, legality or information accuracy is not satisfactory.

3. Enquiry, screening and approval

A submitted form is an enquiry or application, not an acceptance, booking, employment offer or guarantee of service. Customers provide government ID and a selfie for private verification. Companion applicants provide government ID, selfie, short video/liveness verification, police character certificate and references. Verification confirms submitted information only; it does not guarantee character, intentions or behaviour.

Companion applications are reviewed privately. Where possible, Just Company aims to complete an initial review within 2–3 business days, subject to document completeness and additional checks. If approved, the applicant is added to the approved Independent Companion list. Approval does not guarantee hours, bookings, income or a particular customer.

4. Matching and coordination

There is no public customer or companion directory at launch. Just Company does not provide open browsing, swiping or unrestricted member messaging. Each opportunity is considered based on the request, activity, location, timing, boundaries, availability, screening and safety suitability. A companion may accept or decline an opportunity. A booking is confirmed only after suitability, companion acceptance, payment and the agreed details are complete.

After a customer and companion are mutually matched and the booking is approved, Just Company may offer each person an optional introduction or profile image of the other person. This requires explicit consent from both parties. Images are not public profiles, are shared only for that approved booking and must not be reused or redistributed without fresh consent. Verification selfies, government ID, video verification, police certificates and other screening records are never shared for introductions.

5. Locations and prohibited services

At launch, sessions take place only in selected public locations or approved facilities, such as established malls, cafés, parks, libraries, community centres or cultural/shopping spaces. No private-home visits, customer-provided transportation, alcohol sessions, romantic or sexual services, nursing, medication administration, bathing, toileting, dressing, lifting, wound care, therapy, diagnosis or emergency response are provided.

6. Customer responsibilities

  • Provide truthful information and disclose relevant boundaries, accessibility needs, safety concerns and the identity of the person attending.
  • Attend at the confirmed location and time, behave respectfully and follow the agreed activity and service boundaries.
  • Do not pressure, threaten, harass, stalk, exploit, record, photograph or privately contact a companion outside the approved process.
  • Do not request prohibited care, private-home visits, transport, romance, sexual contact, alcohol or financial assistance.
  • Do not pay a companion directly. Report any request for direct payment or money immediately.

7. Companion responsibilities

Companions are independent service providers, not employees of Just Company. The separate Independent Companion Services Agreement governs their screening, conduct, payment, confidentiality, safety duties and opportunity acceptance. No hours, bookings or income are guaranteed. A companion may decline an opportunity and may leave if they feel unsafe.

8. Fees and payment

The customer service fee is TT$95 per companionship hour for one to five hours: TT$95, TT$190, TT$285, TT$380 or TT$475. It covers the companion’s time and service coordination only. Meals, drinks, tickets, personal purchases, travel and approved expenses are separate and must be discussed before confirmation. No cash payments are accepted. Confirmed customers pay Just Company by approved Trinidad and Tobago bank transfer or PayPal.

9. Cancellations and refunds

  • Before a companion accepts, the customer may withdraw the enquiry without creating a confirmed booking.
  • More than 48 hours before a confirmed session, Just Company will generally consider a refund or rescheduling, less unrecoverable costs.
  • Between 24 and 48 hours, Just Company may offer a rescheduling credit or partial refund depending on the circumstances and costs incurred.
  • Under 24 hours, the fee may be retained because the companion reserved the time.
  • If Just Company cancels or cannot provide a suitable replacement, the customer may choose an available rescheduling option or a refund of the affected service fee, subject to payment-provider limitations.
  • Approved expenses already incurred or committed may be treated separately. Refund timing depends on the payment provider.

10. Safety and emergencies

Just Company uses private review, selected locations, screening, controlled coordination, check-in/check-out and a safety word or emergency phrase. These measures reduce risk but cannot eliminate it. Just Company is not an emergency service. In an immediate emergency in Trinidad and Tobago, contact Police at 999 or Ambulance at 811 first, then notify Just Company when safe.

11. Privacy and records

Verification and booking information is handled according to the Privacy Policy. Information is shared only as necessary for review, coordination, safety, payment, legal compliance or incident handling. Customers and companions must not copy or disclose each other’s private information.

12. Suspension and termination

Just Company may pause, refuse, suspend or end participation for inaccurate information, failed screening, harassment, threats, boundary violations, unsafe conduct, prohibited services, privacy breaches, payment problems or legal concerns. A customer or companion may withdraw, but confirmed cancellation terms may still apply.

13. Limits and acknowledgement

Just Company coordinates a service; it does not guarantee a particular outcome, relationship, companion, availability or complete protection from harm. Users remain responsible for their own decisions and conduct. By submitting an enquiry or application, the person confirms that they have read these boundaries, will provide accurate information and understand that a form submission is not a confirmed booking or approval.

14. Contact and changes

Questions or complaints may be sent to justcompany@priscion.com. Just Company may update these terms as the service develops and will publish the current version on this page.
