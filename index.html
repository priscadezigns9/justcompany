What is Just Company?

Just Company is a managed, non-medical companionship service for adults in Trinidad and Tobago. We arrange thoughtful company for conversation, walks, café visits and other suitable activities in approved public or facility locations.

Is this nursing, medical care or an emergency service?

No. Companions do not provide nursing, medical tasks, personal care or emergency response. If someone needs urgent help, contact the appropriate emergency service.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions begin in approved public or facility locations. Private-home visits and customer-provided transportation are not offered at launch.

How much does companionship cost?

Customer sessions are TT$95 per hour for one to five hours. Any travel, meal or activity expense is discussed and agreed before the booking.

How are companions screened?

Just Company manages the application and screening process, including identity and safety checks before a companion is approved for opportunities.

Can a companion or customer end a session?

Yes. Either person may leave if they feel unsafe or uncomfortable. Check-in, check-out and a safety word are part of the session safeguards.

How do I make an enquiry?

Use the Make a private enquiry button to tell us what kind of company is needed, when and where. We will review the request and follow up with suitable next steps.

How are privacy and safety handled?

Requests and personal information are reviewed privately. Sessions begin in approved public or facility locations, with controlled communication, limited information sharing, check-in and check-out procedures and a safety word. Visit our Safety and Privacy page to learn more.
