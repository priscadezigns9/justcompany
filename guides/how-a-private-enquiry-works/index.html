What happens after you make a private enquiry?

A clear, thoughtful guide to arranging companionship in Trinidad and Tobago—from your first request to a confirmed session.

Reaching out is the first step. You do not need to have every detail worked out before you enquire. Whether you are asking for yourself or for an adult family member, your request helps Just Company understand what kind of companionship you have in mind and whether it may be suitable. An enquiry is private, but it is not yet a confirmed booking or payment.

1. Tell us what you’re looking for

The private enquiry form asks who the companionship is for and what sort of time together would feel right. You can share a preferred activity—such as conversation, a walk and talk, a café visit, reading, games, or a hobby or cultural activity—along with a preferred date, time, duration, and venue type.

You can also note preferences, boundaries, or anything relevant to the request. If you have not chosen an exact venue or activity yet, you can say so. The purpose is to give Just Company enough context to review the request and discuss possible next steps.

2. Just Company reviews the request

Just Company is a managed companionship service, not an open marketplace. The team reviews each enquiry for suitability and availability, then contacts you about the next step. A request is not automatically accepted, and a particular companion or preference cannot be guaranteed. Companions also decide whether to accept or decline an opportunity.

Submitting the form does not confirm a booking or payment. The team may need to clarify details before it can say whether the request can proceed.

3. Agree on the activity and setting

At launch, sessions take place only in selected public community locations or approved facilities. Private-home visits are not available. Sessions are also limited to daylight hours during the initial launch. The venue, timing, activity, and any travel arrangements are reviewed before a session can be confirmed. Requests outside the agreed service area need separate review and approval.

Before a booking is confirmed, the activity, location, time, duration, boundaries, expenses, and contact process should be agreed. If a proposed venue or arrangement is unsuitable, Just Company may decline it or discuss another option.

A note about expenses

The service fee covers the companion’s time and service coordination. Travel, meals, drinks, tickets, and personal purchases are optional and are not automatically included. Any expense arrangement should be discussed and agreed before confirmation; a companion may accept or decline the opportunity.

4. Wait for clear confirmation

Once the request, availability, and practical details have been reviewed, Just Company will contact you about the next steps. Do not treat the form submission as a booking: wait until Just Company confirms the session and its arrangements. Current service-fee and payment information is provided through the enquiry and confirmation process.

This pause is intentional. It gives everyone a chance to understand the plan, raise questions, and decide whether the arrangement feels appropriate before it goes ahead.

5. Know what to expect during a session

Before the session, the agreed activity and boundaries should be clear. The companion checks in on arrival using the method provided by Just Company, and checks out at the end. If either person feels unsafe, uncomfortable, or pressured, either may end the session immediately and move to a safe public place. Any concern should be shared with Just Company when it is reasonably safe to do so.

What Just Company is—and is not

Just Company is a managed, paid, non-medical companionship service built around conversation and shared activities. It is not nursing, home care, medical advice, therapy, emergency response, a transport service, or dating. If you need medical, personal-care, transport, or emergency support, please contact a suitable provider for that service.

No process can remove every risk. Screening, public locations, clear boundaries, and check-ins are intended to reduce avoidable risk, not to guarantee that an incident will never occur. For the full safeguards and service boundaries, read the Safety and Privacy page.

If the service sounds suitable, you can begin with a private enquiry. You do not need to have every detail settled; share what you know, and the team can review the request with you.
