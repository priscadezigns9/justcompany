What to expect from your first companionship outing

A calm, practical look at how an outing is planned, what is agreed beforehand, and how the session is handled.

A first outing does not need to be elaborate. It can be a conversation over a café visit, a walk and talk, reading, games, or a shared hobby. What matters is that the activity feels comfortable, the practical details are clear, and both people understand the boundaries before meeting.

Start with what would feel comfortable

When you make a private enquiry, you can describe the kind of companionship you are looking for and the activities that interest you. The form includes options such as conversation and company, walking, a café or restaurant visit, reading or games, and hobbies or cultural activities. You can also share preferences, boundaries, and the kind of support you hope the session will provide.

You do not need to have every detail decided before reaching out. Just Company reviews the request and contacts you about suitability, availability, and possible next steps. A form submission is not a confirmed booking, and a particular companion preference cannot be guaranteed.

Choose an appropriate setting

At launch, Just Company sessions are held only in selected public community locations or approved facilities. Private-home visits are not available. The initial launch also accepts sessions only in daylight. The proposed place, activity, and timing are reviewed before anything is confirmed; requests outside the agreed service area need separate review and approval.

Just Company’s enquiry form includes public options such as cafés or restaurants, parks or walking areas, libraries or community centres, and cultural or shopping spaces. A location being public does not make every journey or surrounding area risk-free, so the specific venue and timing should be agreed in advance.

Transport is not part of the outing by default

Each person should arrange their own reliable transportation. A customer does not transport the companion, and the companion does not transport the customer unless a separate written safety arrangement has been approved.

Agree on the practical details first

Before the session is confirmed, the activity, venue, date, time, duration, boundaries, expenses, and contact process should be clear. Ask questions if anything is uncertain. The companion sees the location and expense arrangement before accepting the opportunity, and may decline it.

The Just Company service fee covers the companion’s time and service coordination. Travel, meals, drinks, tickets, and personal purchases are optional and are not automatically included. Any offer to cover a companion expense should be discussed and agreed before the booking is confirmed. No cash payments are accepted; confirmed customers pay Just Company by the methods described in the enquiry process.

During the session

Companionship is meant to feel respectful and natural. Conversation, shared interests, a walk, or another agreed activity can give the outing its shape. There is no requirement for the session to become more than what both people agreed to. Personal boundaries should be respected throughout.

On arrival, the companion checks in using the method provided by Just Company. At the end, the companion checks out and reports any concern, incident, or material change. Either person may end the session immediately if they feel unsafe, uncomfortable, or pressured. They should move to a safe public place and contact Just Company when reasonably safe.

Know the service boundaries

Just Company is a managed, paid, non-medical companionship service. It is not nursing, home care, medical advice, therapy, emergency response, a transport provider, or dating. If you need any of those services, contact a suitable provider instead. Screening, public locations, clear limits, and check-ins reduce avoidable risk, but cannot guarantee that an incident will never occur.

For full details on locations, boundaries, check-ins, privacy, and safety limits, read the Safety and Privacy page.

A good first outing begins with clear expectations—not pressure to make it perfect. Share what you are looking for, ask questions, and wait for Just Company to confirm the arrangement before making plans.
