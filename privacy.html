Privacy Policy.

How Just Company handles identity, booking, screening and safety information.

Effective date: 22 September 2026

Privacy principle: Just Company collects sensitive information only for a defined service, screening, safety, payment or legal purpose. Government ID, selfies, video verification and screening records are not public profiles.

1. Information we collect

Customer information

  • Name, phone, email, WhatsApp or Telegram details and preferred contact method.
  • Whether the request is for the customer, a family member or someone they support.
  • Requested activity, date, time, duration, town, venue, preferences, boundaries and notes.
  • Government ID, selfie and, where applicable, emergency-contact information.
  • Payment, booking, cancellation, communication and incident records.

Companion information

  • Name, email, phone, location, availability, interests, experience and references.
  • Government ID, selfie, short video/liveness verification, police character certificate and supporting documents.
  • Screening decisions, agreement acceptance, opportunity history, check-in/check-out and payment records.

2. How we use information

  • Review and verify enquiries and applications.
  • Assess suitability, location, timing, boundaries and safety.
  • Coordinate opportunities and communicate confirmed details.
  • Process payments, refunds, expenses and records.
  • Manage check-in/check-out, safety concerns, incidents and complaints.
  • Meet legal, accounting, fraud-prevention and record-keeping obligations.
  • Improve forms, policies and service operations.

We do not sell personal information or create public customer or companion directories. We do not use verification documents for advertising.

3. Verification and video

Video verification is a short private liveness and identity-assurance step for companion applicants. It is not public, is not a character score and does not guarantee safety. Access is limited to people who need it for screening, safety, legal or administrative purposes.

4. Sharing

We may share the minimum necessary information with approved service personnel, payment providers, secure storage or technology providers, professional advisers, emergency services or law-enforcement authorities where required or reasonably necessary for safety or law. A companion receives only the customer information necessary for an approved opportunity. Government ID, police certificates, selfies and video are not shared with customers or companions.

5. Storage and retention

We use access controls and private storage for sensitive files. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, active service administration, disputes, safeguarding, accounting, legal obligations and limitation periods. When information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or de-identified where practical. Exact retention periods may vary by record type and will be documented as the service matures.

6. Security limits

We take reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Users should use accurate contact information, avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information and report suspected misuse promptly.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask what information we hold, request correction, ask about deletion or restriction, withdraw consent where processing depends on consent, or ask a question about sharing and retention. Some records may need to be retained for safety, legal, payment or dispute purposes. Contact justcompany@priscion.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

8. Communications and cookies

We may contact you about your enquiry, application, screening, booking, payment, safety or service administration. We do not promise that every channel is private after a user chooses to use it. The website may use essential browser storage or basic technical logs to operate securely; we do not use verification files for tracking or targeted advertising.

9. Children and sensitive situations

Just Company is intended for adults. Do not submit a child’s information or someone else’s identification without lawful authority and a legitimate service purpose. Just Company is not a medical, mental-health or emergency service.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service, law or safeguards change. The current version will be posted here. Questions, requests and concerns can be sent to justcompany@priscion.com.
