Safety and privacy

Safety first.

Just Company is designed around deliberate coordination, clear boundaries and selected public meeting places.

Effective date: 22 September 2026

No system can remove every risk. Our goal is to reduce avoidable risk through layered screening, controlled coordination, public locations, clear limits, check-ins and rapid escalation of concerns.

1. What Just Company is

Just Company is a managed, paid, non-medical companionship service. It is not a dating app, hookup service, therapy service, nursing service, home-care service, transport provider, emergency-response organisation or replacement for family, clinical or social services.

2. Launch location rules

At launch, sessions may occur only in selected public locations or approved facilities that are suitable for the activity and time. Examples include established malls, cafés, parks, libraries, community centres and cultural or shopping spaces. Private-home visits are not available at launch. The customer does not provide transportation for the companion, and the companion does not transport the customer unless a separate written safety arrangement is approved.

Just Company may reject a venue, time, route or activity without disclosing operational screening criteria that could weaken safety. A venue approval is not a guarantee that the surrounding area or journey is risk-free.

3. Screening and verification

Customers

Customer enquiries require contact information and private identity verification through government ID and a selfie. An emergency contact is required when the request is for the customer themselves. Just Company may request clarification, additional information or a private video check before confirming a first session where appropriate.

Companions

Companion applicants must be adults living in Trinidad and Tobago and provide government ID, a selfie, a short private video/liveness verification, a police character certificate and references. Screening is reviewed privately. The video helps confirm liveness and identity; it does not prove character or guarantee behaviour.

Approval is not automatic. Where possible, initial review is targeted within 2–3 business days, subject to complete documents and additional checks. Approved applicants are added to the approved Independent Companion list, but approval does not guarantee hours, bookings or income.

4. Controlled coordination

  • There is no public customer or companion directory at launch.
  • Customers and companions do not browse or swipe through an open marketplace.
  • Just Company reviews the request and offers a suitable opportunity privately.
  • Companions may accept or decline each opportunity.
  • Personal contact details, home addresses and unnecessary private information are not shared.
  • Communication should remain through approved channels unless Just Company specifically authorises otherwise.

5. Before, during and after a session

  • Before confirmation, the activity, location, time, duration, boundaries, expenses and contact process are agreed.
  • On arrival, the companion checks in using the method Just Company provides.
  • At the end, the companion checks out and reports any concern, incident or material change.
  • A private safety word or emergency phrase may be provided to the companion before the session.
  • Either person may end the session immediately if they feel unsafe, uncomfortable or pressured. They should move to a safe public place and contact Just Company when reasonably safe.

6. Evening and night-session policy

During the initial launch, Just Company accepts sessions only in daylight and does not approve sessions scheduled to continue after dark. Requests for evening or night sessions are not automatically approved. They may be considered only after Just Company has completed a documented risk review and confirmed that the necessary safety procedures can be supported for that time.

Any future evening or night booking must be at a pre-approved, staffed, well-lit public venue with other people present, and must end before the venue closes. The activity, route and timing must be agreed in advance; each person must consent to the arrangement; and each must arrange their own reliable transport. Customer-provided transport, private homes and overnight sessions remain prohibited. Just Company must also be able to support the agreed check-in/check-out and escalation process. If any of these conditions cannot be met, the request will not be approved. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee safety.

7. Boundaries and prohibited conduct

No person may request or provide nursing, medical advice, diagnosis, medication administration, bathing, toileting, dressing, lifting, transfers, wound care, therapy, emergency response, private transportation, romantic or sexual services, alcohol sessions or unapproved private-home visits. Harassment, threats, stalking, coercion, discrimination, intoxication, recording without consent, financial exploitation and requests for direct payment are prohibited.

8. Reporting

Report threats, harassment, fake identity concerns, unsafe venues, boundary violations, direct payment requests, missing check-ins, accidents or any uncomfortable conduct to Just Company at justcompany@priscion.com. Include the reference, date, location and factual details if safe to do so. Just Company may pause a booking, restrict communication, preserve relevant records, contact the parties, remove a person from the approved list or make a report to authorities where required.

9. Emergencies

Just Company is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee immediate physical assistance. If there is immediate danger in Trinidad and Tobago, call Police at 999 or Ambulance at 811 first. Move to a safe public place, alert venue staff or trusted people nearby, and notify Just Company when safe. Do not wait for the chatbot or Just Company to respond to an emergency.

10. Privacy and evidence

Identity documents, selfies, videos, police certificates, emergency details and incident records are private and access-controlled. They are not public profile content and are not shared with companions or customers unless necessary and lawful for a specific safety or service purpose. Relevant records may be preserved for safeguarding, complaints, payment disputes, legal compliance or law-enforcement requests. See the Privacy Policy.

11. Honest limits

Screening and public locations reduce avoidable risk but do not guarantee a person’s honesty, intentions or behaviour and do not guarantee that an incident will not occur. Customers and companions must use their own judgement, respect boundaries and leave when uncomfortable. Just Company may update this procedure as experience, professional advice and local requirements develop.
