What you need to qualify

Just Company is building a careful network of reliable adults who value respectful, real-life connection.

  • Be at least 18 years old and live in Trinidad and Tobago.
  • Be dependable, respectful and comfortable spending time with adults from different backgrounds.
  • Pass identity and safety screening.
  • Provide the required identification, selfie, police certificate and references.
  • Accept the Independent Companion Services Agreement and follow Just Company’s privacy and safety rules.

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or an opportunity. Applications are reviewed privately, and suitable applicants are contacted about the next step.
